### Welcome to AccessTransit!

[**AccessTransit**](https://access-transit.onrender.com/) is a civic tech initiative developed by Eileen Rodriguez, Kelly Xiong Chen, and Jahmari Maxwell, under the team name **VKEbility**, as part of the **Marcy Lab School**. Our mission is to create a full-stack application that delivers real-time transit accessibility updates, empowering all riders- regardless of mobility, language, or ability- to navigate New York City’s public transit system safely, confidently, and with ease.

#### Project Overview

[AccessTransit](https://github.com/VKEbility/access-transit) is designed to fill critical gaps in the accessibility of NYC's subway system. By leveraging crowdsourced data, the app provides accurate, real-time updates on elevator and escalator statuses, helping riders plan their trips more effectively. Additionally, our platform offers multilingual support to ensure that important alerts and information are accessible to non-English speakers, creating a more inclusive commuting experience.

#### Key Features

- **Real-time Accessibility Updates**: Stay informed about the operational status of elevators and escalators in NYC’s subway system.
  
- **Crowdsourced Data**: Empowering riders to report elevator/escalator outages and updates, providing the community with the most reliable, up to date information.

- **Multilingual Alerts**: Critical transit alerts are translated into multiple languages, ensuring no rider is left behind due to a language barrier.
